Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Jurisdiction: Pandharpur / Solapur, Maharashtra, India

Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before reserving or using transportation services provided by Pandharpur Car Cab ("we", "us", "our"). By booking a vehicle through our website, phone dispatch, or office at Bhakti Nivas, you agree to comply with these terms.

1. Scope of Services

Pandharpur Car Cab operates commercial point-to-point passenger transport, station transfers, temple pilgrimage packages, and outstation rentals. Services are provided with commercial tourist vehicles driven by licensed commercial drivers holding valid RTO permits.

2. Bookings and Reservation Confirmation

  • Reservations submitted through our online portal generate a provisional booking voucher with a unique Reference ID.
  • Driver and vehicle registration details (number plate) are dispatched via SMS / phone call 60 to 90 minutes prior to scheduled pickup.
  • During high-rush pilgrim festival days (such as Ashadhi & Kartiki Ekadashi), bookings must be confirmed at least 24 hours in advance.

3. Tariffs, Tolls, and Payments

  • Base Fares: Quoted rates cover vehicle usage, driver fees, and fuel for the specified route or duration.
  • Outstation Minimums: All outstation round-trip bookings are subject to a minimum billing threshold of 250 kilometers per calendar day.
  • Excluded Charges: Government road toll charges, municipality entry taxes, parking fees at monuments/stations, and interstate entry permits are payable directly as per actual receipts.
  • Payment Mode: Payment can be completed in cash or via UPI directly to the driver upon completion of the service.

4. Cancellations and Rescheduling

  • Local Trips: Free cancellation up to 2 hours before scheduled pickup time.
  • Outstation Trips: Free cancellation up to 6 hours before departure time.
  • Driver Dispatched: If a cancellation is requested after a vehicle has already arrived at the pickup location, a minimal arrival fee of ₹200 (local) or ₹500 (outstation) applies.

5. Passenger Responsibilities and Code of Conduct

  • Smoking, consumption of alcohol, or illegal substances is strictly forbidden inside all vehicles in compliance with Maharashtra state laws and the holy sanctity of Pandharpur pilgrimage zone.
  • The maximum number of passengers must not exceed the vehicle's government-approved seating capacity (4 passengers for hatchbacks/sedans, 6-7 passengers for SUVs).
  • Passengers are responsible for their personal belongings. We do not accept liability for items left behind, though lost property retrieved by our chauffeurs is safely kept at our S20 Bhakti Nivas office for recovery.

6. Limitation of Liability

We maintain our fleet to strict mechanical standards. However, Pandharpur Car Cab is not liable for indirect losses, missed train/flight connections, or delays caused by severe traffic congestion, mechanical breakdowns beyond reasonable control, road closures by authorities, or extreme weather conditions. In the event of a vehicle breakdown, we make every reasonable effort to provide an equivalent replacement vehicle in a timely manner.
