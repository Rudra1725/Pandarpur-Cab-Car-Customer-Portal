Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pandharpur Car Cab ("we", "us", or "our"), having its office at S20, Vitthal Rukmini Bhakti Nivas, Pandharpur, Maharashtra 413304, collects, processes, and protects your personal information when you use our website, mobile interface, telephone dispatch, or car rental services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data strictly necessary to fulfill transportation and reservation requests:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone/mobile number, and email address (if provided).
  • Trip Details: Pick-up location, drop-off destination, travel dates, pick-up times, and passenger count.
  • Communication Records: WhatsApp messages, SMS confirmations, or phone call logs regarding dispatch and routing.
  • Technical Data: Standard browser headers, approximate IP-derived location, and browser cookies essential for session maintenance. We do not store sensitive payment card details on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for legitimate business and transportation purposes:

  • To schedule, coordinate, and dispatch drivers and vehicles to your requested pickup point.
  • To send booking confirmation SMS, WhatsApp vouchers, or status updates regarding your driver assignment.
  • To verify booking inquiries submitted through the online customer portal.
  • To maintain financial accounting ledgers and comply with commercial transport regulations in Maharashtra, India.

3. Cookies and Browser Storage

Our website uses standard HTTP session storage and local browser storage strictly for functional purposes (such as retaining your booking reference in the customer portal on your device). We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade customer information to marketing firms or third parties. Information is disclosed solely to:

  • Assigned Chauffeurs: Your name, pickup landmark, and telephone number are shared with your assigned driver solely to execute your trip.
  • Law Enforcement & Regulatory Authorities: Where mandatory under Indian law, Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, or valid legal orders issued by judicial bodies in Solapur District or Maharashtra State.

5. Data Retention and Security Practices

We retain your booking records for 24 months for commercial tax compliance and dispute resolution, after which digital records are deleted or anonymized. We adopt industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards against unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. However, transmission over the public internet or cellular networks cannot be guaranteed as completely secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection principles (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal contact details associated with your phone number in our booking registry.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate trip records.
  • Request erasure of your non-regulatory personal contact details after the completion of your service.
